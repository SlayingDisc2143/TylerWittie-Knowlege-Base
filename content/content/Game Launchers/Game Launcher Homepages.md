# Game Launchers

---
**Game launchers** are software applications that allow players to purchase, download, install, update, and launch video games. Many modern PC games are distributed digitally, making game launchers an important part of PC gaming. Different companies operate their own launchers and digital storefronts. While many of these programs perform similar functions, they can have different game libraries, social features, subscription services, and account systems. Game launchers are a type of [[Software Main Pages|software]] and provide an important connection between games and the computer's [[Hardware Main Page|hardware]].

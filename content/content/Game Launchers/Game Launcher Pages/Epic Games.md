# Epic Games

---

The **Epic Games Store** is a digital storefront and game distribution platform operated by Epic Games. PC players can use the Epic Games Launcher to purchase, download, install, update, and launch games. The launcher provides access to games published by Epic Games as well as games from other developers and publishers. ([[References]]) The Epic Games Launcher is one of several [[Game Launchers]] available for PC gaming and provides an alternative to platforms such as [[Steam]], the [[EA Game Launcher|EA app]], and the [[Xbox App]]. Games downloaded through Epic still depend on the computer's [[Device Drivers]] and [[Hardware Main Page|hardware]] to operate. The Epic Games Launcher provides players with a library where installed and owned games can be accessed. This allows the launcher to act as a central location for managing games associated with the player's Epic account. Some games purchased through Epic may also depend on another company's launcher. For example, [[EA Game Launcher|Electronic Arts]] states that its EA app is required when launching EA titles through Epic or Steam. ([[References]]) This demonstrates how different [[Game Launchers]] can sometimes interact rather than operating completely independently.

## Playing With Friends

Epic Games can also connect with [[Playing games with friends main pages|playing games with friends]]. Many games distributed through the Epic Games Store include online [[Multiplayer Games|multiplayer]] features. Depending on the individual game, players may also have access to [[Cross-Platform Play]], allowing them to play with friends using another supported gaming platform. Players can use different [[Peripheral devices main pages|peripheral devices]] while playing games through Epic, including a [[Keyboard]], [[Mouse]], or [[Controller]], depending on the game.

# EA Game Launcher

---

The **EA app** is Electronic Arts' game platform for PC and Mac. It allows players to find, download, install, and launch games associated with their EA Account. Electronic Arts describes the EA app as its platform for accessing EA games and managing a player's game library. ([[References]]) The EA app replaced EA's older **Origin** platform. Players who previously used Origin were moved to the EA app, with their games and other content associated with their account transferring to the newer platform. ([[References]]) The EA app provides a library where players can access games associated with their EA Account. Games purchased through the application can be downloaded and installed from the player's library. ([[References]]) Like other [[Game Launchers]], the EA app provides a central location for managing games rather than requiring the player to manually organize every game installation. Games launched through the EA app still rely on the computer's [[Hardware Main Page|hardware]], [[Operating Systems]], and [[Device Drivers]].

---
## EA App and Other Launchers

The EA app can also interact with other gaming platforms. Electronic Arts states that the EA app is required to play EA games on PC even when certain EA titles are launched through [[Steam]] or [[Epic Games]]. ([[References]]) This means that a player may occasionally use more than one [[Game Launchers|game launcher]] to access a game. For example, a player could begin launching an EA game through Steam before the EA app opens to provide access to the EA services required by that game.

## Playing With Friends

The EA app includes social features designed to help players connect with friends. EA allows users to connect their EA Account with other gaming platforms and import friends so that they can see what friends are playing. ([[References]]) These features connect the EA app with [[Playing games with friends main page|playing games with friends]]. Depending on the individual game, EA games may support [[Multiplayer Games|multiplayer]] or [[Cross-Platform Play]]. The controls available depend on the game. PC players may use a [[Keyboard]] and [[Mouse]], a [[Controller]], or other supported [[Peripheral devices]].

---
## Origin and the EA App

**Origin** was Electronic Arts' previous PC game platform. EA introduced the EA app as its replacement and moved players' games, cloud saves, and friends information to the newer application. ([[References]]) Understanding Origin is useful when researching older PC games because older instructions or websites may refer to Origin even though the EA app is now EA's current platform.

# Xbox App
---
The **Xbox App** is Microsoft's gaming application for Windows PCs. It allows PC players to discover, install, organize, and launch games associated with Xbox and PC Game Pass. The application also connects Microsoft's gaming services with Windows, allowing players to communicate with friends and access games across supported devices. ([[References]]) The Xbox App is one of several [[Game Launchers]] available on PC and provides an alternative to platforms such as [[Steam]] and [[Epic Games]].

## Games and Game Pass

One of the major features of the Xbox App is its integration with Xbox Game Pass. Players with an appropriate subscription can browse available games and install supported PC games directly through the application. ([[References]]) The Xbox App also provides access to cloud gaming for supported subscriptions and games. Instead of running the game entirely using the player's local [[Hardware Main Page|computer hardware]], cloud gaming streams the game to the computer over the internet.

The Xbox App connects closely with [[Playing games with friends main pages|playing games with friends]]. Microsoft provides features for communicating with friends across PC, Xbox consoles, and mobile devices. ([[References]]) This makes the application more than simply a way to install games. Friends lists, party communication, invitations, and multiplayer features can all contribute to the social side of PC gaming. Some games also support [[Cross-Platform Play]], allowing PC players to play with people using other supported gaming platforms.

---
## Controllers and Other Inputs

Games installed through the Xbox App can support a variety of [[Peripheral devices main pages|peripheral devices]]. Depending on the individual game, players may use a [[Keyboard]] and [[Mouse]] or a [[Controller]]. Microsoft's Xbox controllers are particularly closely integrated with Windows and Xbox games, giving players the option of using a console-style control method on a gaming PC.

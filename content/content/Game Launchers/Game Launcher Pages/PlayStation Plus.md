# PlayStation Plus
---

PlayStation Plus was Sony's subscription service for streaming and downloading PlayStation games. The standalone PlayStation Now service is no longer offered under that name. Its cloud-streaming functionality became part of Sony's redesigned PlayStation Plus service. For a PC gaming knowledge base, PlayStation Plus is still important because it represents an example of **cloud gaming**, where a game can be processed remotely and streamed to a player's computer rather than running entirely on the player's local [[Hardware Main Page|hardware]].

---

Sony currently provides cloud-streaming features through **PlayStation Plus Premium**. Supported games can be streamed rather than downloaded and processed entirely on the local device. ([[References]]) Sony also documents PlayStation Plus game streaming on PC. When streaming, the player's internet connection becomes particularly important because video, audio, and player inputs must travel between the player and the remote system. ([[References]]) Similar to the services the [[Xbox App]] provides, Sony lists connection requirements for its cloud-streaming services. ([[References]]) Cloud gaming can also connect to [[Playing games with friends main pages|playing games with friends]] because supported games may provide online multiplayer features.

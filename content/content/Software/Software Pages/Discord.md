# Discord
---
**Discord** is a communication application commonly used by gaming communities. Users can communicate through text messages, voice calls, and video while participating in individual conversations or larger community servers. Discord is particularly useful when [[Playing games with friends main page|playing games with friends]] because players can remain in a voice channel while playing a [[Multiplayer Games|multiplayer game]]. Discord servers can also contain separate channels for different games, topics, announcements, and groups of players.

# Device Drivers
---
**Device drivers** are pieces of software that allow an operating system to communicate with the computer's hardware. Components such as the [[GPU]], network adapter, audio devices, and [[Peripheral devices main pages|Peripheral devices]] rely on drivers so that they can properly communicate with the computer.

Drivers are especially important for PC gaming. Graphics card manufacturers such as NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel regularly provide graphics drivers for their hardware. Keeping drivers updated can provide support for newer games, correct problems, and ensure that hardware works correctly with other [[Software Main Pages|software]].

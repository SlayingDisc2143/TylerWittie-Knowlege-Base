# Mouse
---
A **mouse** is one of the primary input devices used with a computer and is commonly paired with a [[Keyboard]] for PC gaming. Moving the mouse across a surface controls the pointer or camera on the screen, while its buttons allow the player to perform different actions. In video games, a mouse is particularly useful for actions that require precise and rapid movement, such as aiming in first-person shooters or selecting units in strategy games. Most computer mice include left and right buttons and a scroll wheel, while gaming mice may include additional programmable buttons. Like other [[Peripheral devices main pages|Peripheral devices]], gaming mice are available with different features depending on how the player intends to use them.

---
## Gaming Mice
A **gaming mouse** is designed with features intended specifically for playing video games. These can include adjustable sensitivity, additional programmable buttons, higher "polling" rates, and software for customizing the device. Mouse sensitivity is commonly measured using **DPI**, or dots per inch. Changing the DPI can affect how far the cursor moves in response to physical movement of the mouse. Many gaming mice allow the player to adjust this setting to match their personal preferences or the type of game being played. ([[References]]) Gaming mice can also include additional buttons that can be assigned to actions that would normally require the [[Keyboard]]. This gives players another way to customize their controls.

## Wired and Wireless Mice

Computer mice can connect to a gaming computer through either wired or wireless connections. A wired mouse typically connects directly through USB, while wireless mice can use Bluetooth or a dedicated wireless USB receiver. Modern gaming mice are available using both connection methods. The best option depends on the player's preferences, computer setup, battery requirements, and the features offered by the individual mouse. The [[Software Main Pages|software]] provided by some mouse manufacturers can also allow players to customize sensitivity, lighting, button assignments, and other settings.

---
## Keyboard and Mouse Gaming

The [[Keyboard]] and mouse combination is one of the most common methods of controlling PC games. The keyboard generally handles movement and other commands while the mouse controls actions such as aiming and camera movement. This control method is particularly common in first-person shooters and strategy games. Other types of games may be more comfortable to play with a [[Controller]], meaning PC players can choose between different [[Peripheral devices main pages|Peripheral devices]] depending on the game they are playing. Games installed through [[Game Launchers]] such as [[Steam]] and [[Epic Games]] can support different input methods depending on how the individual game was developed. A mouse is also important when [[Playing games with friends main pages|Playing games with friends]] online. Competitive multiplayer games often require players to quickly aim, navigate menus, or interact with objects in the game. A [[Keyboard]] can also be used alongside the mouse for text communication, while a Microphone or Voice Chat system can provide another method of communicating with other players. Unlike multiple [[Controller|controllers]], connecting multiple mice to the same computer generally does not provide each local player with an independent control method. Keyboard and mouse gaming is therefore particularly associated with individual PC setups and online [[Multiplayer Games|multiplayer gaming]].

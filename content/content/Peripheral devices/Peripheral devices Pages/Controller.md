# Controllers
---
A **controller** is an input device that allows a player to interact with a video game. Controllers are most commonly associated with video game consoles, but modern controllers can also be connected to a gaming PC. They provide an alternative to using a [[Keyboard]] and [[Mouse]] and can be especially useful for games that were originally designed with console-style controls in mind. Most modern controllers include two analog sticks, directional controls, triggers, shoulder buttons, and several face buttons. Depending on the controller, additional features such as vibration, motion controls, built-in audio, or more advanced haptic feedback may also be available.

## Xbox Controllers

The **Xbox Wireless Controller** is Microsoft's standard controller for Xbox consoles and is also compatible with Windows PCs. It can connect to compatible computers using Bluetooth, Xbox Wireless, or a wired USB connection. Microsoft describes the current controller as having features such as textured grips, a hybrid directional pad, and a dedicated Share button. ([[References]]) Because Xbox controllers are designed to work with Windows, they are a common [[Peripheral devices main pages|peripheral device]] for PC gaming. A controller can provide a more console-like experience when playing games on a gaming computer.

## PlayStation Controller

Sony's **DualSense wireless controller** is the standard controller associated with the PlayStation 5. The DualSense includes features such as haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, a built-in microphone, motion sensing, and a touch pad. Sony also officially supports connecting the DualSense to compatible Windows PCs and other devices through USB or Bluetooth. ([[References]])
The DualSense demonstrates how controllers can provide more than simple button inputs. Its haptic feedback and adaptive triggers can change the physical feedback provided to the player when supported by a game.

## Controllers and Local Gaming

Controllers are particularly useful when [[Playing games with friends main pages|playing games with friends]] locally. Instead of requiring every player to have a separate gaming computer, some games allow multiple controllers to be connected to the same system. This type of multiplayer is commonly called **local multiplayer** or **couch multiplayer**. Multiple people can play together while sharing the same computer or display. For example, one player may use a [[Keyboard]] while another uses a controller, or several controllers may be connected to the same computer. Controllers therefore provide an important connection between [[Peripheral devices main pages|Peripheral devices]] and [[Playing games with friends main pages|playing games with friends]]. They give players another method of interacting with games while also making certain types of local multiplayer easier to set up.
## Connecting Controllers

Modern controllers can typically connect either through a wired USB connection or wirelessly. The Xbox Wireless Controller supports Xbox Wireless and Bluetooth connections in addition to wired connections, while the PlayStation DualSense supports compatible devices through USB and Bluetooth. ([[References]]) The connection method and available controller features can vary depending on the game, operating system, and controller being used. Players should check whether a game supports controllers before expecting all of a controller's features to work.

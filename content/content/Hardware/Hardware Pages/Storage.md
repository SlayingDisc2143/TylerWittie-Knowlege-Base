# Storage 
--- 
Storage is where the operating system, games, programs, and personal files are stored. Modern computers commonly use SSDs because they are much faster than traditional hard drives. NVMe SSDs can provide faster boot times, loading times, and file transfers. 

## Storage Types
---
### SSD
An SSD (Solid State Drive) is a type of storage device that uses FLASH drives that allow the user to quickly store data permanently without the use of spinning disks. This was a major advancement in computer data storage as the previous Hard Disk Drives were prone to failure. These SATA drives are connected via SATA cables to the [[Motherboard|motherboard]] and somtimes connected to the [[power supply]] (PSU). 

### HDD
An HHD (Hard Disk Drive) is a type of storage device that utilizes spinning disks and a metallic arm that etches data on to said disks, driven my magnetic arm drivers. HDDs are substantially  slower than flashable drives, the information is not limited to a mechanical medium. The physical movements of the HDD are a major factor in transfer speeds. This chokes online downloads and reduces overall efficiency. Like the SSD the drive is connected via SATA connectors to the [[Motherboard|motherboard]] and the power supply to power the mechanical components within.

### NVMe
An NVMe (Non-Volatile Memory Express) is a type of storage device that is directly attached to a socket located on the [[Motherboard|motherboard]]. This allows for super fast transfers internally due to the direct integration with the [[Motherboard|motherboard]]. Due to the NVMes integration these drives are what is mainly used (and recommended for most PCs) for storing essential programs such as the OS (operating system).

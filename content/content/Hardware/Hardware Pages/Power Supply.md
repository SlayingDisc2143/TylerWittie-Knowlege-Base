# Power Supply 
---
The power supply unit, or PSU, provides electricity to all of the computer's components. The PSU needs to provide enough wattage for the [[CPU]], [[GPU]], [[Hardware Main Page|Case Fans]],  and other hardware. It is also a good idea to have some extra wattage available for future upgrades. 

## Power Supply Naming
---
Power supply's have a rating system that signifies the amount of power the PSU (Power Supply Unit) can output and it also correlates to the efficiency that the unit outputs power. According to INTEL "This efficiency rating is determined by performance in a 115-volt system, and the ratings are further increased as you go up the ladder of precious metals.

These include:
- 80 PLUS  
- 80 PLUS Bronze
- 80 PLUS Silver
- 80 PLUS Gold
- 80 PLUS Platinum
- 80 PLUS Titanium

The more efficient your PSU, the less power it uses, and the less heat it generates. That said, higher efficiency usually means a higher price, so you’ll want to find the balance that works for you."

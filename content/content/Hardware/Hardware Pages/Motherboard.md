## Motherboard 
---
The motherboard is the main circuit board that connects all of the computer's components. Parts such as the [[content/Hardware/Hardware Pages/CPU|CPU]], [[RAM|RAM]], [[GPU]], [[Power Supply]], and [[Storage]] connect to the motherboard. When choosing a motherboard, it is important to make sure that it is compatible with the CPU, RAM, case, and other components being used.

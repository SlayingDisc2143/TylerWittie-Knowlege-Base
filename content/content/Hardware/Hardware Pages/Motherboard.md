## Motherboard 
---
The motherboard is the main circuit board that connects all of the computer's components. Parts such as the [[content/Hardware/Hardware Pages/CPU|CPU]], [[RAM|RAM]], [[GPU]], [[Power Supply]], and [[Storage]] connect to the motherboard. When choosing a motherboard, it is important to make sure that it is compatible with the CPU, RAM, case, and other components being used. 

## Main Components
---
### Slots
---
Slots are located throughout the motherboard and hold some of the necessary components to run a computer. This can include: 
- CPU Socket Connector
- PCIE slots 
- DIMM memory slots
- and PCI slots for expansion cards
### Ports
---
Ports are located on the outer and back parts of the board that allow for peripheral devices to be connected. There are many plugin types that have many differing connector types. This may include:
- USB
- SATA
- Ethernet
- USB-C
- Audio out jacks
### Pins
---
Pins are located on the outermost parts of the motherboard that connect to the [[Power Supply|power supply]] and other devices like fans, case monitors for viewing task management software.

# Multiplayer Games
---
**Multiplayer games** allow two or more people to participate in the same game. Players can compete against each other or work together toward a common goal. Multiplayer can take place online, through [[LAN Gaming]], or locally on the same computer. Games such as **Minecraft**, **Counter-Strike 2**, **Overwatch 2**, **Fortnite**, **Call of Duty**, and **Grand Theft Auto Online** are examples of games that allow players to play together online. Different games use different methods to connect players. **Minecraft** can use [[LAN Gaming]] or [[Dedicated Servers]], while games such as **Counter-Strike 2** use online servers to host matches. **Fortnite** and **Overwatch 2** support [[Cross-Platform Play]], allowing friends on different supported platforms to play together. Other games, such as **Cuphead**, provide local cooperative multiplayer where two people can play together using devices such as a [[Controller]] or [[Keyboard]].

## Playing With Friends

Multiplayer games are an important part of [[Playing games with friends main page|playing games with friends]]. Platforms such as [[Steam]], [[Epic Games]], the [[EA Game Launcher|EA app]], and the [[Xbox App]] provide access to many multiplayer games and can include friends lists, invitations, and other social features. Multiplayer games can use [[Dedicated Servers]], [[Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer]], [[LAN Gaming]], or [[Cross-Platform Play]] depending on how the game was developed.

## Examples

- **Minecraft** – LAN, online servers, and Realms
- **Counter-Strike 2** – Competitive online multiplayer
- **Overwatch 2** – Team-based online multiplayer
- **Fortnite** – Online multiplayer and cross-platform play
- **Grand Theft Auto Online** – Open-world online multiplayer
- **Call of Duty** – Competitive and cooperative multiplayer
- **Cuphead** – Local cooperative multiplayer
- **Rocket League** – Online multiplayer and cross-platform play

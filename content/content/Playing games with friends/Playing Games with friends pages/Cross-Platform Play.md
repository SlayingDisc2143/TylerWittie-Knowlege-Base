# Cross-Platform Play
---
**Cross-platform play**, commonly called **cross-play**, allows people using different gaming platforms to play the same [[Multiplayer Games|multiplayer game]] together. For example, a PC player may be able to play with friends using an Xbox or PlayStation console. Cross-play makes [[Playing games with friends main pages|playing games with friends]] easier because everyone does not necessarily need to own the same gaming system.

---
Cross-platform support depends on the individual game and its developers. Some games allow players across PC and multiple consoles to play together, while others limit multiplayer to specific platforms. Services such as the [[Xbox App]], [[Steam]], and [[Epic Games]] can also be involved in connecting players and managing their accounts. Cross-play has become an important feature of online gaming because it allows larger groups of players to play together regardless of their preferred platform.

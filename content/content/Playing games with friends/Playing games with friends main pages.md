# Playing Games with Friends
---
Playing games with friends is a major part of PC gaming and allows players to interact with other people either cooperatively or competitively. Modern games provide several different ways for players to connect, whether they are playing over the internet or together on the same local network.
## Ways to Play With Friends

- [[Multiplayer Games]] – Games designed to allow multiple players to participate in the same game at the same time.

- [[Dedicated Servers]] – Separate servers that host multiplayer games and allow multiple players to connect to the same session.

- [[Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer]] – Allows players to connect directly to each other, often with one player's computer acting as the host.

- [[Cross-Platform Play]] – Allows players using different platforms, such as PC and consoles, to play together.

- [[LAN Gaming]] – Allows computers on the same local network to connect and play games together.

### Online Gaming
Most modern multiplayer games use an internet connection to allow players to play with people in different locations. Game launchers such as [[Steam]], [[Epic Games]], and [[EA Game Launcher]] can provide features such as friends lists, game invitations, matchmaking, and other tools for connecting players.
Some games use [[Dedicated Servers]] to host matches, while others use [[Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer]] to establish connections between players. The system used depends on how the game's multiplayer functionality was designed.
### Local Gaming
Not all multiplayer gaming requires players to connect through an online service. [[LAN Gaming]] allows computers connected to the same local network to communicate with each other and participate in multiplayer games.
Local multiplayer can also involve multiple people using different [[Controller|controllers]] or other [[Peripheral devices main pages|Peripheral devices]] on the same computer.
### Communication
Communication is another important part of playing games with friends. Voice Chat allows players to speak with one another while playing and can make coordinating in multiplayer games much easier. Players may use audio [[Peripheral devices|]] to communicate. Some games include their own communication systems, while external software can also be used alongside a game. Playing games with friends combines gaming hardware, software, networking, and [[Peripheral devices main pages|Peripheral devices]] to create shared experiences between players.

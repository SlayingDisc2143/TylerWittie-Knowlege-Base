## Hardware

This includes components such as the GPU, CPU, RAM, storage, motherboard, and other peripheral devices that are required or wanted to create an enjoyable computing and gaming experience. Each component serves a different purpose and can affect the overall performance of the computer.

### Necessary Hardware 
---
There are many different types of hardware that are required for an individual to run games and complicated computing tasks. Among the parts listed below there are very important things to consider when picking the parts for a PC. 

#### Motherboard 
---
The motherboard is the main circuit board that connects all of the computer's components. Parts such as the CPU, RAM, graphics card, and storage devices connect to the motherboard. When choosing a motherboard, it is important to make sure that it is compatible with the CPU, RAM, case, and other components being used. 
#### CPU 
---
The CPU, or Central Processing Unit, is responsible for processing instructions and performing calculations for the computer. A more powerful CPU can improve performance in games, multitasking, programming, video editing, and other demanding applications. Important things to consider include the number of cores, clock speed, power requirements, and compatibility with the motherboard. It is also important to consider the type of CPU needs to correlate to the Motherboards model, as some motherboards require different CPUs for compatibility. #### Case 
--- 
The computer case houses and protects all of the components. A case needs to support the size of the motherboard and have enough room for the graphics card, power supply, storage, and cooling equipment. Good airflow is also important when choosing a case.

#### GPU 
--- 
The GPU, or Graphics Processing Unit, is responsible for processing and displaying graphics. It is especially important for gaming because it renders the images and effects displayed on the monitor. Important things to consider when choosing a GPU include performance, VRAM amount, power consumption, and the resolution you want to play at. The resolution is limited by the monitor you have and its capability to display the resolution the GPU was specified for. If you have a monitor that can only run 1080p and a GPU that renders on a 4K display, you will only see a 1080p render. 

#### RAM 
--- 
RAM, or Random Access Memory, temporarily stores information that the computer is actively using. Having enough RAM allows the computer to run games and multiple programs without slowing down. The amount, speed, and type of RAM should also be compatible with the motherboard and CPU. 

#### Storage 
--- 
Storage is where the operating system, games, programs, and personal files are stored. Modern computers commonly use SSDs because they are much faster than traditional hard drives. NVMe SSDs can provide faster boot times, loading times, and file transfers. 

#### Power Supply 
---
The power supply unit, or PSU, provides electricity to all of the computer's components. The PSU needs to provide enough wattage for the CPU, GPU, and other hardware. It is also a good idea to have some extra wattage available for future upgrades.
